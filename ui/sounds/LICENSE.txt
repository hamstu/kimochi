From Kenney (www.kenney.nl), the "Interface Sounds" and "UI Audio" packs. Re-encoded from
Ogg Vorbis to MP3 (WebKit cannot play Ogg), with a short light reverb tail (the dry sound unchanged, a short room under it) added, and renamed.

Interface sounds

sound bank (`earshot sfx render <id>`), then mixed to mono, levelled against the scan sounds and
encoded to MP3. No recordings are involved.
