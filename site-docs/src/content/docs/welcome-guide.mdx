The first time Kimochi opens, a short welcome guide sets up what it needs. Every step can be changed
later in Settings, and you can open the guide again from **Settings → General → Help → Welcome guide**.

<Screenshot id="welcome-intro" alt="The welcome guide's first page: the Kimochi icon, Read and play in Japanese, and a Get started button." width={640} />

## The steps

| Step | What it does | Shown on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Allow screen recording** | Lets Kimochi capture the window you read. | macOS |
| **Allow Accessibility** | Optional: lets a click on your source bring that exact window forward. | macOS |
| **How should Kimochi read text?** | Picks the [OCR engine](/reading/ocr-engines/). | All |
| **Choose a pop-up dictionary** | Picks and downloads a [dictionary engine](/lookups/dictionaries/). | All |
| **Set up …** | Sets up that engine (Jitendex for Yomitan). | All |
| **Connect your browser's Yomitan** | Sets up the Yomitan API instead, when you chose it. | All |
| **Add Kimochi to your Steam library** | Adds Kimochi to Steam for Gaming Mode. | Steam Deck, Desktop Mode |
| **You're ready to read** | Recaps the loop and offers a test lookup. | All |

In Steam Deck Gaming Mode the capture and Accessibility steps are skipped: Gaming Mode shares the
game screen on its own.

## OCR engine

Local engines keep every scan on this computer. Cloud engines need a connection and may need
credentials. Google Lens is selected to begin with; see [OCR engines](/reading/ocr-engines/) for
the differences.

## Dictionary engine

Kimochi looks words up with a pop-up dictionary engine. Choose one and Kimochi downloads it from its
official release (Yomitan is recommended). You can switch at any time in
**Settings → Dictionary**.

<Screenshot id="welcome-engine" alt="The Choose a pop-up dictionary step, with Yomitan selected and a Download 15 MB button." width={640} />

If you skip it, nothing will look words up until you choose and download a dictionary in
**Settings → Dictionary**. Scanning and the Log still work.

### Set up Yomitan

Yomitan needs at least one dictionary, and the step offers three ways to get one. Choose one,
then press the button at the bottom right:

- **Install Jitendex** (chosen to start with) downloads the Jitendex Japanese–English dictionary and enables it in your
  current Yomitan profile; existing dictionaries and preferences are kept. It carries on in the
  background if you press **Continue while installing**.
- **Choose your own**, then **Open dictionary settings**, opens Yomitan's settings, to import dictionaries or a settings backup from
  Yomitan in your browser.
- **Do it later**, then **Continue**, moves on; add a dictionary any time in **Settings → Dictionary**.

<Screenshot id="welcome-dictionaries" alt="The Set up Yomitan step: Install Jitendex, Choose your own, and Do it later." width={640} />

Hachidori offers a set of recommended dictionaries the first time its settings open. 10ten works
straight away and downloads its full dictionaries in the background.

## You're ready to read

The last step recaps the loop: **choose and crop**, **scan**, **look up** (hold <kbd>Shift</kbd> and
hover a word, or use controller reading navigation; press **B** to go back to your game). With
Yomitan, **Test a lookup of 日本語** checks that your dictionary answers.
