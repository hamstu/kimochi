More questions are answered on [kimochiapp.com/faq](https://kimochiapp.com/faq).

### Is it only for games?

No. Kimochi reads any Japanese on your screen: visual novels, manga, videos, web pages. For manga, try the
[Manga OCR](/reading/ocr-engines/#manga-ocr) engine.

### Is Kimochi free?

Yes. Everything but **Kimochi Cloud** is free and needs no account. Kimochi Cloud is optional and
adds translation, AI explanations, read aloud, cloud OCR and sync; see
[Accounts and plans](/cloud/).

### Which platforms does it run on?

macOS (Apple silicon, macOS 14 or later), Windows (x64 and ARM64) and Linux (x64 and ARM64), plus
Gaming Mode on the Steam Deck. macOS support is the most mature. See [Installation](/install/).

### Does Kimochi send my screen to the cloud?

Only if you choose an online OCR engine. Google Lens (the default) and Kimochi cloud OCR send the
captured region for recognition. [Local engines](/reading/ocr-engines/) don't. Kimochi has no analytics,
telemetry, crash reporting or ads. See [Privacy](/help/privacy/).

### Do I need an account?

Only for Kimochi Cloud. You can sign up with email, Google or Discord, and download or delete your
data from your account page.

### Do I need OBS or a browser extension?

No. Kimochi has built-in screen and window capture with a crop area, and Yomitan is built in. You can
still use the Yomitan in your browser through the [Yomitan API](/lookups/dictionaries/#yomitan-api).

### Does it work with Anki and texthookers?

Yes. Kimochi adds cards to Anki through AnkiConnect ([Anki](/lookups/anki/)). Kimochi can hook a
Windows game's text itself with its built-in [Text Hooker](/reading/text-hooker/) (LunaHook or
Textractor, downloaded the first time), on Windows and, through Proton or Wine, on Linux and the
Steam Deck. On any system, if you already hook text out of a visual
novel, point Kimochi at your texthooker over WebSocket, or copy lines to the clipboard
([Texthookers and clipboard](/reading/texthookers/)).

### Does it work with a controller or on the Steam Deck?

Yes. In Steam Deck Gaming Mode, press R4 (the upper right back button) to open the controller menu and
scan, step to a word with the D-pad to see its dictionary entry, and press B to go back to the game. See
[Controller](/controller/) and [Gaming Mode](/controller/gaming-mode/).

### Is it open source?

Yes, under the GPL-3.0. The source is on [GitHub](https://github.com/hamstu/kimochi).

### Is there a Kimochi community?

Yes: [Kimochi's Discord](https://discord.gg/vdMge6TBZQ). Ask for help, share setups and hear about new
releases first. Hamish is there too.

### How do I report a bug or give feedback?

Ask in [Kimochi's Discord](https://discord.gg/vdMge6TBZQ), or open an issue on
[GitHub](https://github.com/hamstu/kimochi/issues). See
[Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting/#still-stuck) for what to include.
