More questions are answered on [kimochiapp.com/faq](https://kimochiapp.com/faq).

### Is it only for games?

No. Kimochi reads any Japanese on your screen: games, visual novels, manga, YouTube videos,
streams, web pages, anything with Japanese text in it. For manga, try the
[Manga OCR](/reading/ocr-engines/#manga-ocr) engine.

### Is Kimochi free?

Yes, with no account and no limits on the core app: the overlay, Auto OCR, on-device and online OCR
engines, the Log, Sessions, the Notebook, dictionaries, Anki cards and Gaming Mode. **Kimochi Cloud**
is optional and adds translation, AI explanations, read aloud, cloud OCR and sync; see
[Accounts and plans](/cloud/).

### Which platforms does it run on?

macOS (Apple silicon, macOS 14 or later), Windows (x64 and ARM64) and Linux (x64 and ARM64), plus a
Gaming Mode for the Steam Deck. macOS support is the most mature, and Steam Deck Gaming Mode is in
early testing. See [Installation](/install/).

### Does Kimochi send my screen to the cloud?

Only if you choose an online OCR engine. Google Lens (the default) and Kimochi cloud OCR send the
captured region for recognition. For nothing to leave your computer, choose an on-device engine:
Apple Live Text (macOS), MeikiOCR, Manga OCR or OneOCR (Windows). Kimochi has no analytics,
telemetry, crash reporting or ads. See [Privacy](/help/privacy/).

### Do I need an account?

No. You only need one for Kimochi Cloud. You can sign up with email, Google or Discord, and download
or delete everything from your account page at any time.

### Do I need OBS or a browser extension?

No. Kimochi has built-in screen and window capture with a crop area, and Yomitan is built in. You can
still use the Yomitan in your browser through the [Yomitan API](/lookups/dictionaries/#yomitan-api).

### Does it work with Anki and texthookers?

Yes. Kimochi adds cards to Anki through AnkiConnect ([Anki](/lookups/anki/)). If you already hook
text out of a visual novel, point Kimochi at your texthooker over WebSocket, or copy lines to the
clipboard ([Texthookers and clipboard](/reading/texthookers/)).

### Does it work with a controller or on the Steam Deck?

Yes, though Steam Deck support is early. In Gaming Mode, press R4 (the upper right back button) to scan, step to a
word with the D-pad to see its dictionary entry, and press B to go back to the game. See
[Controller](/controller/) and [Gaming Mode](/controller/gaming-mode/).

### Is it open source?

Yes, under the GPL-3.0. The source is on [GitHub](https://github.com/hamstu/kimochi-tauri).

### Is there a Kimochi community?

Yes: [Kimochi's Discord](https://discord.gg/vdMge6TBZQ). Share what you're reading, swap setups and
dictionaries, ask for help, suggest features and hear about new releases first. Hamish is there too.

### How do I report a bug or give feedback?

Ask in [Kimochi's Discord](https://discord.gg/vdMge6TBZQ), or open an issue on
[GitHub](https://github.com/hamstu/kimochi-tauri/issues). See
[Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting/#still-stuck) for what to include.
