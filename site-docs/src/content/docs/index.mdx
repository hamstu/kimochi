## What Kimochi does

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Capture and OCR" icon="laptop">
    Pick a game window or a whole screen, crop it to the dialogue, and press Scan or turn on
    **Auto OCR**. Choose from online and on-device OCR engines. [Sources](/reading/sources/) ·
    [OCR engines](/reading/ocr-engines/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="The overlay" icon="seti:image">
    Kimochi can draw the recognised text back over the game, so you can hover any word right
    where it is. [The overlay](/reading/overlay/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Pop-up dictionaries" icon="open-book">
    Built-in Yomitan, Hachidori or 10ten, downloaded from their official releases, or the Yomitan
    in your browser. [Dictionaries](/lookups/dictionaries/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="The Log" icon="document">
    Every line lands in a Log you can scroll back through, with a session and reading stats per
    game, translation and explanations. [The Log](/log/the-log/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Notebook" icon="pencil">
    Keep the characters, places and things of a story. Names you add light up in the Log as you
    read; import a cast from VNDB. [Notebook](/log/notebook/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Anki" icon="add-document">
    Make cards through AnkiConnect, from the dictionary pop-up. [Anki](/lookups/anki/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Controller and Steam Deck" icon="star">
    A radial menu and word-by-word reading on a gamepad, including Steam Deck Gaming Mode
    (experimental). [Controller](/controller/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Texthookers" icon="random">
    Already hooking text out of a visual novel? Feed Kimochi over WebSocket or the clipboard.
    [Texthookers and clipboard](/reading/texthookers/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Kimochi Cloud" icon="rocket">
    Optional: cloud OCR, read aloud, translation, AI explanations and sync between computers.
    [Accounts and plans](/cloud/)
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## How it fits together

1. **Pick the game.** Choose a window or screen in the sidebar, and add a crop area if you like.
2. **Scan.** Press **Scan now** (<kbd>⇧⌘8</kbd> on a Mac, <kbd>Ctrl+Shift+8</kbd> elsewhere), or turn on Auto OCR.
3. **Read.** The text shows up in the Log, and over the game when the overlay is on.
4. **Look it up.** Hover a word to see its meaning, hear it, save it to Anki, and keep going.
