Wherever Japanese appears, Kimochi's dictionary pop-up is a hover away. The pop-up is the
[dictionary engine](/lookups/dictionaries/) you chose, so its look, audio and Anki button are that
engine's.

## Which key to hold

| Engine | To look a word up |
| --- | --- |
| **Built-in Yomitan**, **Hachidori**, **Yomitan API** | Hold <kbd>Shift</kbd> and hover the word. |
| **10ten** | Just hover: 10ten looks words up on hover by default. To make it wait for a key, change its pop-up settings in **Settings → Dictionary → Open 10ten settings**. |

The same key works in the Log and over the game with the overlay.

## In the Log

Hold <kbd>Shift</kbd> (or just hover, with 10ten) over a word in any line. The pop-up shows the entry for the longest word
that starts there, with its reading, definitions and (for most engines) audio and an Anki button.

## On screen, with the overlay

With the [overlay](/reading/overlay/) on, hover a word right where it is on screen, with the same
key held. The word you're on is highlighted and the pop-up opens beside it.

## Inside a pop-up

With Built-in Yomitan or the Yomitan API, hold <kbd>Shift</kbd> over a word inside a pop-up (in a
definition or an example sentence) to look it up in another pop-up on top. You can keep going: Built-in
Yomitan goes as deep as its **Maximum number of child popups** setting (under **Allow scanning popup
content** in its settings), the Yomitan API up to five pop-ups. Click back
in an earlier pop-up to close the ones above it. This works in the Log and
over your game, with the mouse.

## With a controller

Open the [controller menu](/controller/#the-controller-menu) (click both sticks, or
<kbd>⇧⌘Space</kbd> / <kbd>Ctrl+Shift+Space</kbd>) and choose **Scan** (scan, then start at
the first word) or **Read along** (step through the last scan):

| Control | Action |
| --- | --- |
| D-pad ◀ ▶ | Previous / next word |
| D-pad ▲ ▼ | Previous / next line (column, for vertical text) |
| **Y** | Step by word or by character |
| Left stick | A free cursor that looks up the character it rests on |
| **A** | Into the pop-up, where the D-pad chooses a button (Add to Anki, Play audio) and **A** presses it |
| **X** | Play the word's audio |
| **LB** / **RB** | Previous / next definition in the pop-up |
| Right stick | Scroll the pop-up |
| **B** | Back to the game |

Words are split the way the dictionary sees them: with Built-in Yomitan, the longest dictionary match
with deinflection, so 食べられなかった is one stop, not seven. The Yomitan API uses its own tokenizer,
and Hachidori and 10ten a general word splitter. On the keyboard, the arrows,
<kbd>Return</kbd>, <kbd>Space</kbd> (by word or by character) and <kbd>Esc</kbd> do the same while
the menu is open. **Settings → Game Mode** can show the dictionary full screen instead; see
[Pop-up or full screen](/controller/#pop-up-or-full-screen).

**Recent text** in the controller menu lists your recent scans; pick one to read its words again.

## Notebook names

Names you keep in the [Notebook](/log/notebook/) are recognised in the Log and shown as chips. Click
one to see its card: what it is, its nicknames and notes, and **Open in Notebook**.
