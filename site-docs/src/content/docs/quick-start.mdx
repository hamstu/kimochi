This assumes you have [installed Kimochi](/install/) and gone through the
[welcome guide](/welcome-guide/). Everything you need is in the main window's sidebar:

<Screenshot id="sidebar-callouts" alt="The sidebar with callouts: 1 live preview of the source, 2 crop to the dialogue, 3 scan once, 4 Auto OCR scans whenever text changes, 5 Show overlay draws the text over the source, 6 the OCR engine, 7 the pop-up dictionary." width={558} />

<Steps>

1. **Choose a source.** Open what you want to read (a game, a manga reader, a video, a web page),
   then in Kimochi's sidebar choose **Choose a source** (or **Change source** once one is set) →
   **Window or screen**, and pick its window.

   <Screenshot id="source-grid" alt="The source picker: Screens and App windows, each with a live thumbnail, the current one badged Current." width={720} />

2. **Crop to the text (optional).** **Crop area → Set crop area…** lets you draw a box around
   where the text appears, such as a dialogue box or a video's subtitles, so menus and other text
   are left out. See [Sources and crop](/reading/sources/#crop-area).

3. **Scan.** Press **Scan now** in the sidebar, or the Scan shortcut from anywhere:
   <kbd>⇧⌘8</kbd> on a Mac, <kbd>Ctrl+Shift+8</kbd>
   on Windows and Linux. The text appears as a new line in the Log.

   <Screenshot id="scan-button" alt="The sidebar with the Scan now button highlighted, showing its shortcut." width={240} />

4. **Or let it scan for you.** Turn on **Auto OCR**: Kimochi watches the source and reads it each
   time the text changes and settles. See [Auto OCR](/reading/auto-ocr/).

5. **Look up a word.** Hold <kbd>Shift</kbd> and hover a word in the Log to see its meaning. Turn on
   **Show overlay** to draw the text over what you’re reading and hover words right there.

   <Screenshot id="overlay-demo" alt="Words boxed over a visual novel's dialogue, with a dictionary pop-up under the cursor." width={560} />

6. **Keep reading.** Every line stays in the Log for this game's session, with your reading time
   and speed in the footer.

</Steps>
