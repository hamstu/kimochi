You don't need an account: Kimochi works fully without one. An account is where **Kimochi Cloud**
lives, the optional paid plan for the extras that cost money to run.

| Free, no account | Kimochi Cloud |
| --- | --- |
| The overlay, Auto OCR and Mini mode | Translation of any line, right in the app |
| On-device and online OCR engines | On-demand AI explanations |
| The Log, Sessions and the Notebook | Read aloud in ElevenLabs voices |
| Yomitan, Hachidori, 10ten or your own Yomitan | Cloud OCR with Google Vision |
| Anki cards | Sync of your sessions and Notebook across computers |
| Gaming Mode on the Steam Deck | |

Kimochi Cloud costs **$6 a month**, **$60 a year** (two months free) or **$125 once** for lifetime,
with a **14-day free trial**. See [plans on kimochiapp.com](https://kimochiapp.com/#open-source).

## Signing in

You can sign up with email, Google or Discord on [kimochiapp.com](https://kimochiapp.com/account/sign-up).
To sign the app in:

<Screenshot id="settings-account" alt="Settings, Account when signed out: Kimochi cloud, with Sign in or create an account." width={760} />

<Steps>

1. In Kimochi, open **Settings → Account** and choose **Sign in or create an account**.

2. Your browser opens on kimochiapp.com and Kimochi shows a code, such as `K7QM-3XPA`. Sign in on the
   website if you need to.

3. Check that the browser shows the same code, then choose **Yes, sign in Kimochi**. Only approve if
   you just chose Sign in in Kimochi and it shows this exact code.

4. Kimochi comes back to Settings → Account on its own. **Open Kimochi** on the website brings it
   forward.

</Steps>

<Screenshot id="settings-account-linking" alt="Settings, Account while signing in: Your code K7QM-3XPA, Waiting for confirmation, Open browser again and Cancel." width={760} />

Codes work for 10 minutes, once. If the browser didn't open, or you closed it, choose
**Open browser again**. You can see and sign out linked apps on your
[account page](https://kimochiapp.com/account) under **Kimochi apps**.

## Your account in the app

Signed in, **Settings → Account** shows your plan and **This month**'s usage of cloud OCR, read
aloud, translation, explanations and sync storage, with **Manage account** to open the website.
Without Kimochi Cloud it offers **See plans**; start a trial on the website and Kimochi picks it up
within a few minutes (or press **Refresh**).

<Screenshot id="settings-account-signedin" alt="Settings, Account when signed in: the account on this computer, Kimochi Cloud plan, and This month's usage meters." width={760} />

## More than one account

**Accounts on this computer** lists each account you have signed in, plus **Local**: this
computer's own Log, without an account, not synced. Each account keeps its own sessions, Notebook,
presets and preferences; **Switch** swaps them, and nothing is merged or deleted. **Add account**
signs in another.

Signing out switches Kimochi to Local; the account's data stays on this computer, ready when you sign
in again. **Remove from this computer…** signs out and deletes that account's data here (what has
synced stays in your account).

## Managing your account

On [kimochiapp.com/account](https://kimochiapp.com/account) you can change your profile and sign-in
methods, manage your plan, see and sign out linked apps, download or delete your synced data, and
delete your account. Deleting removes everything straight away and signs out every linked app.
