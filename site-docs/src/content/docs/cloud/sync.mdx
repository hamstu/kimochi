Signed in with Kimochi Cloud, your **sessions, Notebook, presets and preferences** stay on this
computer and sync in the background with your other computers signed in to the same account, even
after time offline. Your DeepL key, other secrets and this computer's own settings (window
positions, capture source and the like) never leave it.

There is no switch: signed in means syncing. Kimochi syncs a few seconds after a change, when a
window comes forward, at start and every minute. Offline, it notes what changed and tries again a
minute later.

<Screenshot id="settings-account-conflicts" alt="Settings, Account, Sync: Sync now, Storage, two Conflicts with Restore and Dismiss, Sync log and Export your data." width={760} />

| Row | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Sync now** | Syncs straight away. |
| **Storage** | How much of your sync storage is in use. |
| **Conflicts** | When something changes on two computers at once, the newer change wins and the other is kept here. **Restore** makes it current again; **Dismiss** drops it. |
| **Sync log** | **View sync log** opens a timestamped record of what Sync sent, received, merged and kept (**Copy all** to share it). |
| **Export your data** | **Export…** saves everything this account has synced as a ZIP of plain JSON files and images. Also on the website, under Manage account. |

## First sign-in

The first time an account is used on a computer, Kimochi copies this computer's Local Log into it if
the account has nothing synced yet, so a new sign-up keeps its work. Otherwise it fills it from the
server.

## Without an account

Without an account, nothing syncs and there is no export in the app. Your Log, Notebook and settings
are in Kimochi's [data folder](/reference/data/); back that folder up to keep a copy.
