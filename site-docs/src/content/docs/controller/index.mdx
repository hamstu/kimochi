Kimochi reads gamepads natively, so buttons work while the game has focus. Gamepad support is on by
default (**Settings → Shortcuts & Controls → Gamepad → Enable gamepad support**).

Kimochi has been tested with a PlayStation 4 controller on macOS, Windows and Linux, and any
standard gamepad should work. Until a button press lights up in Settings, it notes that gamepad
support hasn't been verified on your system yet. If your controller doesn't respond, let us know
in [Kimochi's Discord](https://discord.gg/vdMge6TBZQ) or
[open an issue](https://github.com/hamstu/kimochi-tauri/issues), with its make and model.

## The controller menu

Click both sticks (**LS + RS**) or press <kbd>⇧⌘Space</kbd> / <kbd>Ctrl+Shift+Space</kbd>
to open Kimochi's radial menu over the game. Aim with the left stick or step with the D-pad, press
**A** to choose and **B** to close. Every way out gives the game its focus back.

| Item | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Scan** | Scans, then starts word-by-word reading at the first word. |
| **Read along** | Word-by-word reading of the last scan (needs a scan first). |
| **Auto OCR** | Turns [Auto OCR](/reading/auto-ocr/) on or off. |
| **Recent text** | Your recent scans; pick one to read it again. |
| **Kimochi** | Shows the main window. |
| **OCR settings** | Opens Settings → OCR & Overlay. |

Pick a menu chord your game doesn't use; you can change it in
**Settings → Shortcuts & Controls → Shortcuts → Controller menu**.

## Reading word by word

After **Scan** or **Read along**, Kimochi steps through the text one word at a time in reading order,
with the dictionary pop-up open on the current word. A bar at the bottom shows the buttons, and
**View** opens a sheet with every control.

| Control | Action |
| --- | --- |
| D-pad ◀ ▶ | Previous / next word |
| D-pad ▲ ▼ | Previous / next line (column, for vertical text) |
| **Y** | Step by word or by character. By character, a lookup starts at that character: for a word split in the wrong place. |
| Left stick | A free cursor that looks up whatever character it rests on; the D-pad steps on from there |
| **A** | Into the pop-up, to use its buttons (or look the word up again, when the pop-up has none) |
| **X** | Play the word's audio |
| **LB** / **RB** | Previous / next definition |
| Right stick | Scroll the pop-up |
| **View** | Show all controls |
| **B** | Back to the game (or back to Recent text) |

In the pop-up, the D-pad chooses a button (such as Add to Anki or Play audio) and **A** presses it.
Add to Anki is chosen first, so **A**, **A** adds the word. **B** goes back to the text.

## Pop-up or full screen

**Settings → Game Mode → Dictionary in Read along** chooses how Read along shows the dictionary:

- **Pop-up** (the default): the dictionary's pop-up beside the word.
- **Full screen**: the scanned text across the top of a dimmed screen with one word lit, and its
  dictionary entry below. ◀ ▶ move word to word, the right stick scrolls, and **B** goes back to
  the game. With the Yomitan API, **LB** / **RB** step through the word's entries and **A** adds the
  entry to Anki; Built-in Yomitan, Hachidori and 10ten show their own pop-up filling that space,
  and **A** moves into it.

It works with every dictionary.

## In Kimochi's windows

When a Kimochi window is focused, the D-pad and left stick move between controls, **A** activates
the focused one (a text field opens an on-screen controller keyboard), **B** goes back or closes a
dialog, and the right stick scrolls.

## Settings

**Settings → Shortcuts & Controls** has the gamepad settings:

- **Shortcuts**: a gamepad button or combination for **Scan** and **Controller menu**, beside the
  keyboard shortcut.
- **Controller menu**: the buttons for **Select** (A) and **Back** (B).
- **Reading**: the buttons for **Word or character** (Y), **Play audio** (X), **Previous definition**
  (LB), **Next definition** (RB) and **Show all controls** (View). Two actions on the same button
  are pointed out.
- **Connected gamepads**: every pad Kimochi sees; **Set as primary** picks the one it listens to.
- **Button test**: buttons light up as you press them.

For a game where a button advances the text, **Settings → OCR & Overlay → Scan after a button press**
scans a moment after you press it. See [Auto OCR](/reading/auto-ocr/#scan-after-a-button-press).
