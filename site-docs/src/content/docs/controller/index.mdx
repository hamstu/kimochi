Kimochi reads gamepads natively, so buttons work while the game has focus. Gamepad support is on by
default (**Settings → Shortcuts & Controls → Gamepad → Enable gamepad support**).

Kimochi has been tested with a PlayStation 4 controller on macOS, Windows and Linux, and any
standard gamepad should work. On Windows, Xbox-style pads are read with XInput. The docs name
buttons the Xbox way (**A**, **B**, **LB**); with a DualSense or DualShock, Kimochi shows the
PlayStation names instead (✕ ○ □ △, **L1**/**R1**, **Options**). If your controller doesn't respond, let me know
in [Kimochi's Discord](https://discord.gg/vdMge6TBZQ) or
[open an issue](https://github.com/hamstu/kimochi/issues), with its make and model.

## The radial menu

Click both sticks (**LS + RS**; on a Steam Deck, press the upper right back button, **R4**) or
press <kbd>⇧⌘Space</kbd> / <kbd>Shift+F9</kbd> to open Kimochi's radial menu over the game. Aim with the left stick or step with the D-pad, press
**A** to choose and **B** to close.

<Screenshot id="radial-menu" alt="The radial menu over a visual novel: Scan at the top, then Read along, Auto OCR (Off), Recent text, Kimochi and Settings." width={720} />

| Item | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Scan** | Scans, then starts word-by-word reading at the first word. With an OCR source. |
| **Read along** | Word-by-word reading of the last scan or hooked line. |
| **Auto OCR** | Turns [Auto OCR](/reading/auto-ocr/) on or off. With an OCR source. |
| **Text Hooker** | The hooked text and the card's settings. While [text hooking](/reading/text-hooker/#with-a-controller-steam-deck). |
| **Recent text** | Your recent lines; pick one to read it again. |
| **Kimochi** | Shows the main window. |
| **OCR settings** / **App settings** | Opens Settings → OCR (or, while text hooking, Settings). |

Pick a menu chord your game doesn't use; you can change it in
**Settings → Shortcuts & Controls → Shortcuts → Radial menu** (or **Settings → Overlay → Radial menu**).

### Making it your own

**Settings → Shortcuts & Controls → Radial menu** (also on **Settings → Overlay**) shows the menu as
it looks over a game, with an OCR source or while text hooking:

- Drag an item round the ring to move it.
- Click one to give it a shortcut of its own (keys, a controller button or combination, or both)
  that runs it without opening the menu, or to remove it. **Add item** puts back one you removed,
  and **Reset to default** puts back the menu Kimochi ships with.
- **Base colour**, **Accent colour** and **Base opacity** change how it looks. The accent follows
  the overlay's highlight colour until you pick one.

<Screenshot id="settings-radial-menu" alt="Settings, Shortcuts & Controls, Radial menu: the ring with its actions, then Base colour, Accent colour and Base opacity." width={760} />

With [Pause the game while reading](/reading/overlay/#pausing-the-game) on, the game freezes while the
menu is open.

## Reading word by word

After **Scan** or **Read along**, Kimochi steps through the text one word at a time in reading order,
with the dictionary pop-up open on the current word. A bar at the bottom shows the buttons, and
**View** opens a sheet with every control.

| Control | Action |
| --- | --- |
| D-pad ◀ ▶ | Previous / next word |
| D-pad ▲ ▼ | Previous / next line (column, for vertical text) |
| **Y** | Step by word or by character. By character, a lookup starts at that character: for a word split in the wrong place. |
| Left stick | A free cursor that looks up whatever character it rests on; the D-pad steps on from there |
| **A** | Into the pop-up, to use its buttons (or look the word up again, when the pop-up has none) |
| **X** | Play the word's audio |
| **LB** / **RB** | Previous / next definition |
| Right stick | Scroll the pop-up |
| **View** | Show all controls |
| **B** | Back to the game (or back to Recent text) |

In the pop-up, the D-pad chooses a button (such as Add to Anki or Play audio) and **A** presses it.
Add to Anki is chosen first, so **A**, **A** adds the word. **B** goes back to the text.

## Pop-up or full screen

**Settings → Overlay → Game Mode** chooses how Read along shows the dictionary:

- **Pop-up** (the default): the dictionary's pop-up beside the word.
- **Full screen**: the scanned text across the top of a dimmed screen with one word lit, and its
  dictionary entry below. ◀ ▶ move word to word, the left stick points at a word to look it up,
  the right stick scrolls, and **B** goes back to the game. With the Yomitan API, **LB** / **RB** step through the word's entries and **A** adds the
  entry to Anki; Yomitan, Hachidori and 10ten show their own pop-up filling that space,
  and **A** moves into it.

It works with every dictionary.

Words are split before you step through them, and a split can come out wrong. Point the left
stick at the character a word really starts on and the lookup starts there (in full screen, with
the Yomitan API, it looks up the whole word); the outline covers everything the dictionary
matched from it.

## Settings

**Settings → Shortcuts & Controls** has the gamepad settings:

- **Shortcuts**: a gamepad button or combination for **Scan** and **Radial menu**, beside the
  keyboard shortcut.
- **Radial menu**: its items and their shortcuts ([above](#making-it-your-own)).
- **Gamepad**: **Enable gamepad support**, and a **Lookup button** to hold in place of the lookup
  key (see [Holding a button instead of Shift](/lookups/looking-up/#holding-a-button-instead-of-shift)).
- **Radial menu buttons**: the buttons for **Select** (A) and **Back** (B).
- **Reading**: the buttons for **Word or character** (Y), **Play audio** (X), **Previous definition**
  (LB), **Next definition** (RB) and **Show all controls** (View).
- **Connected gamepads**: every pad Kimochi sees; **Set as primary** picks the one it listens to.
- **Button test**: buttons light up as you press them.

<Screenshot id="settings-controls" alt="Settings, Shortcuts & Controls: the Scan and Radial menu shortcuts, Lookup key and Look up on hover, and the top of the Radial menu section." width={760} />

For a game where a button advances the text, **Settings → Shortcuts & Controls → Scan after a button
press** scans a moment after you press it. See [Auto OCR](/reading/auto-ocr/#scan-after-a-button-press).

All buttons are listed in [Controller buttons](/reference/controller-buttons/).
