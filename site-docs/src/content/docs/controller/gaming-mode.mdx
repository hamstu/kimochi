On a Steam Deck, Kimochi can run in **Gaming Mode** next to your game. Gaming Mode support is early
and experimental. First [install Kimochi and add it to Steam](/install/steam-deck/).

<Screenshot id="overlay-demo-deck" alt="A game on a Steam Deck screen with Kimochi reading: a dictionary pop-up for 容赦なく over the dialogue, and a Reading badge." caption="Reading on a Steam Deck (from the live demo on kimochiapp.com)." width={640} />

## Starting

1. In Gaming Mode, start **Kimochi** from your library.
2. Start your game.
3. In Kimochi, **Change source** lists your running games by name, with their Steam icons and live
   previews. Choose the game. Kimochi reads it even while Kimochi itself is in front.
4. Set a crop around the dialogue to skip menus and other text.

Gaming Mode shares the game screen on its own, so there is no capture permission or system picker.

## Reading

Back in the game, click both sticks (**L3 + R3**) to open Kimochi's controller menu and choose
**Scan**. Pick a word with the D-pad, press **A** for the reading, and press **B** to go back to the
game. See [Controller](/controller/).

While Kimochi is in front, a **Gaming Mode** notice says which buttons bring up the game menu, with a
**Return to game** button.
