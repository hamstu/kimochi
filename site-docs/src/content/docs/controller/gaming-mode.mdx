On a Steam Deck, Kimochi can run in **Gaming Mode** next to your game, with a layout made for the
Deck's screen, and you read with the controller over the game. First [install Kimochi and add it to Steam](/install/steam-deck/).

<Screenshot id="overlay-demo-deck" alt="A game on a Steam Deck screen with Kimochi reading: a dictionary pop-up for 容赦なく over the dialogue, and a Reading badge." caption="Reading on a Steam Deck (from the live demo on kimochiapp.com)." width={640} />

## Starting

1. In Gaming Mode, start **Kimochi** from your library, then start your game.
2. Kimochi opens on its **Source** tab, which sets you up in three columns:
   - **Game**: **Source** lists your running games by name, with their Steam icons and live
     previews. Choose the game; Kimochi reads it even while Kimochi itself is in front. **Crop**
     sets an area around the dialogue to skip menus and other text.
   - **Reading**: **Scan now**, **Auto OCR**, and the OCR model and dictionary.
   - **Next**: what to do now. Press the **STEAM** button and choose your game to go back to it;
     Kimochi keeps running. Then press your game-menu button (shown there) to read.
3. **Log**, **Notebook**, **Anki** and **Settings** are tabs across the top.

Gaming Mode shares the game screen on its own, so there is no capture permission or system picker.
Kimochi's window is used by touch (tap, and drag to scroll); a controller press in it says so.

If you play two games, Kimochi follows the one in front, with each game's own crop area. The overlay
always shows over your game, and only over it: its highlights go while Kimochi, Steam's menus or
another game is in front.

## Reading

Back in the game, press **R4** (the upper right back button) to open Kimochi's controller menu and choose
**Scan**. Step to a word with the D-pad and its dictionary entry opens; **A** moves into it to add
it to Anki or play its audio, and **B** goes back to the game. For a bigger dictionary on a TV or
handheld, choose **Full screen** in **Settings → Game Mode**. See [Controller](/controller/).

The controller menu is a ring of large icons around a centre that names the action you're pointing
at. It, the reading hints and Recent text follow your **Theme** (light or dark) in
**Settings → General**.
