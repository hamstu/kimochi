On a Steam Deck, Kimochi can run in **Gaming Mode** next to your game, with a layout made for the
Deck's screen, and you read with the controller over the game. First [install Kimochi and add it to Steam](/install/steam-deck/).

<Screenshot id="overlay-demo-deck" alt="A visual novel on a Steam Deck-sized screen with Read along on 今日: its dictionary pop-up above the dialogue and the controller hints along the bottom." caption="Read along on a Steam Deck." width={640} />

## Starting

1. In Gaming Mode, start **Kimochi** from your library, then start your game.
2. Kimochi opens on its **Source** tab, which sets you up in three columns:
   - **Game**: **Source** lists your running games by name, with their Steam icons and live
     previews. Choose the game; **Crop**
     sets an area around the dialogue to skip menus and other text.
   - **Reading**: **Scan now**, **Auto OCR**, and the OCR model and dictionary.
   - **Next**: what to do now. Press the **STEAM** button and choose your game to go back to it.
     Then press your game-menu button (shown there) to read.
3. **Log**, **Notebook**, **Anki** and **Settings** are tabs across the top.

Kimochi's window is used by touch (tap, and drag to scroll).

If you play two games, Kimochi follows the one in front, with each game's crop area.

## Reading

Back in the game, press **R4** (the upper right back button) to open Kimochi's controller menu and choose
**Scan**. Step to a word with the D-pad and its dictionary entry opens; **A** moves into it to add
it to Anki or play its audio, and **B** goes back to the game. For a bigger dictionary on a TV or
handheld, choose **Full screen** in **Settings → Overlay → Game Mode**. See [Controller](/controller/).
