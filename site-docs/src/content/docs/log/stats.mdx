Kimochi keeps a **reading history**: every line you read, with when you read it and what it came
from. The **Stats** tab in the main window (<kbd>⌘4</kbd> / <kbd>Ctrl+4</kbd>) is drawn from it.

<Screenshot id="stats" alt="The Stats tab: a 13-day streak over a year's heatmap, Today, This week, All time and Speed, then the last 30 days' characters per day." width={900} />

## The Stats tab

From the top:

- **The heatmap**: a year of reading days, darker for days you read more, with your current
  streak, your longest and how many days you've read.
- **Today**, **This week** and **All time**: characters, reading time and lines; **Speed** is
  characters an hour, over all time.
- **The range** (**7 days**, **30 days**, **Year** or **All time**): characters or time per day (per
  week over longer ranges), your reading speed, the hours of the day you read, each session with its
  characters, time, speed and when you last read it, and the kanji you met most, with how many were
  new in that range.

The menu at the bottom right chooses which sessions count: **All sessions** by default, or some of
them, including sessions you've deleted.

Reading time follows the rules in **Settings → Log & Sessions → Stats**, the same as the Log's
footer: **Max time per line** (5 minutes) is the most one line can count, **Ignore skipped lines**
leaves out lines that flash by, and **Day starts at** (4 am) decides which day late-night reading
belongs to. See [Reading stats](/log/sessions/#reading-stats).

## The reading history

The history is separate from the Log. Clearing a session's lines, the Log's **Keep at most** limit
and deleting a session leave it alone; editing a line in the Log changes it there too, and deleting
a line (a misreading) takes it out. With Kimochi Cloud, [Sync](/cloud/sync/) brings it to your
other computers.

## Exporting it

In the Log's **⋯** menu, **Export reading history** saves the whole history as a file:

| Format | For |
| --- | --- |
| **Lines for exSTATic or GSM (CSV)** | [exSTATic](https://github.com/KamWithK/exSTATic), and GameSentenceMiner's exSTATic import: one row a line. |
| **Daily stats for exSTATic (CSV)** | exSTATic's daily totals for each session. |
| **Every detail (CSV)** / **Every detail (JSON)** | Everything Kimochi keeps about each line, for a spreadsheet or your own scripts. |
