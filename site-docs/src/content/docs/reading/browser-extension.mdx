The Kimochi extension reads Japanese in pictures on web pages: manga pages, game screenshots,
comics. Kimochi reads the picture with your OCR engine, adds the lines to the Log, and draws the
text over the picture so your browser's dictionary extension can look it up. Kimochi has to be
running.

It works in Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc and other Chromium browsers. Firefox isn't supported yet.

## Installing it

Open **Settings → Browser Extension**. Until the extension is approved in the Chrome Web Store,
install it by hand:

<Steps>

1. Open your browser's extensions page: `chrome://extensions` (Edge: `edge://extensions`, Brave:
   `brave://extensions`).
2. Turn on **Developer mode**.
3. Choose **Load unpacked** and pick the folder shown in Settings (**Show folder** opens it).
4. Pin Kimochi from the puzzle-piece menu.

</Steps>

After a Kimochi update, click the reload arrow on Kimochi's card on the extensions page.

Your browser also needs a dictionary extension of its own, such as
[Yomitan](https://yomitan.wiki/), [10ten](https://10ten.life/) or Hachidori. Kimochi's built-in
dictionary doesn't reach web pages.

## Reading a picture

1. Click Kimochi in the toolbar, or press <kbd>Alt+K</kbd>.
2. Click a picture. Press <kbd>↑</kbd> while picking to take a bigger area, such as a two-page
   spread, and <kbd>↓</kbd> to narrow it again.
3. Hold <kbd>Shift</kbd> over the text and look words up with your dictionary extension.

The lines also go to the Log, with the picture.

With **Auto** on (the extension's toolbar, on by default), Kimochi reads the picture again when it
changes, including when a reader turns the page or loads the next one. The toolbar also picks the
OCR model and the session.

## Sessions

Each site gets its own session, named for the day the first time. Pick, create or rename it from
the extension's toolbar. Choose **Browser** as the [source](/reading/sources/) to read along in
Kimochi as lines arrive.

**Connect to the browser extension** in Settings turns the connection off.
