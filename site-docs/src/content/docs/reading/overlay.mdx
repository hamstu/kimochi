The overlay draws each scan's text back over the source (a game, a manga page, a video), so you can hover a word where it appears on
screen instead of in the Log. Look for the faint highlights on scanned text: hovering one turns it into a card with the text on it.

<Screenshot id="overlay-demo" alt="Kimochi's overlay over a visual novel: each word of the dialogue boxed, the hovered one highlighted, with its dictionary entry above." width={560} />

## Turning it on

Turn on **Show overlay** in the sidebar (under Auto OCR). The overlay follows the window you read,
and clicks go through it to whatever is underneath, words included, so the game keeps working (see
[Clicking through to the game](#clicking-through-to-the-game)).

<Screenshot id="overlay-switch" alt="The sidebar with the Show overlay switch highlighted." width={240} />

## Looking up words

Hover a word with <kbd>Shift</kbd> held to open its pop-up (see
[Which key to hold](/lookups/looking-up/#which-key-to-hold)). With a controller, open the [controller menu](/controller/#the-controller-menu) and
choose **Scan** or **Read along** to step through the words with the D-pad. See
[Looking up words](/lookups/looking-up/).

## Clicking through to the game

Text on the overlay often sits over the game's own buttons: a dialogue box you click to advance, a
choice, a menu. So clicks go **through** the overlay's text to the game, unless you hold
<kbd>Shift</kbd>:

- **Without Shift**, clicking on scanned text clicks the game underneath.
- **With Shift held**, text lights up as you point at it, and you can look words up, click a word,
  and move or use the [Text Hooker's card](/reading/text-hooker/#the-card-over-the-game). Once text
  is lit it stays clickable while your pointer is on it, even after you let go of Shift.
- **With a dictionary pop-up open**, your next click outside it only closes the pop-up and doesn't
  reach the game. The pop-up itself, and the Text Hooker card's ⋯ menu once it is open, stay
  clickable without Shift.

For the old way, where scanned text lights up as you hover it and takes your clicks, turn off
**Click through text to the game** in **Settings → OCR & Overlay**. You still hold <kbd>Shift</kbd>
to look words up.

On **Linux with Wayland**, Kimochi can't see Shift while another app is in front, so there the
overlay's text always takes your clicks, as it did before.

### The hint bar

While there is text on the overlay, a small bar just below it says what Shift does: **Hold Shift to
look up words and click text** (or, with click-through off, **Hold Shift over a word to look it
up**). It sits just below the crop area or the game's window, or at the bottom of the screen for a
full-screen game. Clicks go through the bar too, except on its **×**, which closes it for good.
**Settings → General → Help → Overlay hints → Show again** brings it back. On Linux with
Wayland, where Shift doesn't gate clicks, the bar isn't shown.

## Options

| Setting | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Group text into regions** | Off | Marks nearby lines with one shared box to hover, instead of a box for each line. |
| **Filter out furigana and small text** | On | Leaves reading aids above kanji out of the overlay and the Log. |
| **Drop lines** | No Japanese | Leaves lines without Japanese out of the overlay and the Log; the same setting as in [The Log](/log/the-log/#which-lines-are-kept). |
| **Click through text to the game** | On | Clicks on scanned text go to the game unless you hold Shift. See [above](#clicking-through-to-the-game). |
| **Exclude overlay from screen recording** | On | Hides the overlay and dictionary pop-ups from screenshots and screen recordings, so Kimochi never reads its own text back. Turn it off to capture them, for example to share a clip. |

## Overlay look

**Settings → OCR & Overlay → Overlay look** changes how the overlay is drawn, with a live preview of
each choice:

| Setting | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Highlight colour** | Indigo | The tint and outline over text you can hover; pick a preset or any colour. |
| **Highlight opacity** | 40% | How strongly the highlight shows before you point at it. |
| **Cards** | Same as Kimochi | The card hovered text turns into: **Same as Kimochi** (follows the app's appearance), **Light**, **Dark**, or **Blend**, which fills each card with the colours behind that word on your screen and picks light or dark text to match, as if the game drew it. |

## Platform notes

- **macOS:** Game Mode throttles the overlay while a full-screen game is in front; Kimochi tells you
  when it happens. See [Install on macOS](/install/macos/#game-mode).
- **Linux on Wayland:** the overlay needs the desktop's layer-shell support or, on GNOME 46, the
  GNOME overlay extension. Without them the switch reads **Overlay unavailable**. See
  [Install on Linux](/install/linux/#the-overlay-on-wayland).
- **Debug overlay** (**Settings → General → Diagnostics**) marks the scanned text boxes over the
  source and opens the Diagnostics window, to check what the OCR found.
