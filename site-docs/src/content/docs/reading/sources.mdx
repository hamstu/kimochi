The **source** is where the Log's lines come from. It sits at the top of the main window's sidebar:
a live preview of the window or screen, its name, and **Crop area** and **Change source**.

<Screenshot id="source-card" alt="The sidebar's source card for a game window, with its live preview, Crop area and Change source buttons, and the Auto OCR and Show overlay switches highlighted." width={240} />

Clicking the card brings the source forward (**Bring to front**, or **Go to full screen** for a
full-screen game). Hover it and the ✕ in the corner clears the source.

## Kinds of source

Open **Change source** (or **Choose a source** when none is set) to switch:

| Source | What it reads |
| --- | --- |
| **Window or screen** | A game window or a whole display, with OCR. |
| **Quick Shot** | No source to choose: the Scan shortcut asks you to draw an area anywhere, then reads it. |
| **Clipboard** | Text copied to the clipboard, for example by a text hooker. |
| **WebSocket** | Lines from a text source on your network. |

**Clear source** forgets the current one, and **Source settings** opens **Settings → Sources**.

## Choosing a window or screen

**Window or screen** opens a grid of **Screens** and **App windows**, each with a live thumbnail.
The current one is badged **Current**. Pick a window (a game, a manga reader, a browser, a video player) to read just that window wherever it
is, or a screen to read everything on it.

<Screenshot id="source-grid" alt="The window and screen picker, with thumbnails of two screens and several app windows." width={720} />

- **macOS** lists windows once Screen Recording is allowed; until then the grid shows
  **Allow Screen Recording…**. See [Install on macOS](/install/macos/#screen-recording).
- **Linux on Wayland** uses your desktop's own dialog instead: **Choose New Source…** opens it,
  **Use Current Source** keeps the last pick. See [Install on Linux](/install/linux/#choosing-what-to-read).
- **Steam Deck Gaming Mode** lists your running games by name, with their Steam icons and live
  previews. See [Gaming Mode](/controller/gaming-mode/).

With **Switch sessions by window name** on (the default), each window you read gets its own
[session](/log/sessions/), created the first time.

## Crop area

A crop area limits scans to part of the source, such as the dialogue box, so menus, HUDs and other
text stay out of the Log. **Crop area → Set crop area…** opens the region editor over the source:

- Drag to draw the box. Hold <kbd>Space</kbd> while drawing to move it.
- Drag inside the box to move it, or its edges and corners to resize it.
- Arrow keys nudge it by one point (<kbd>Shift</kbd> + arrows by ten).
- Press <kbd>Return</kbd>, double-click inside, or choose **Confirm** to keep it; <kbd>Esc</kbd> cancels.

The crop shows as a frame on the live preview. **Change crop area…** edits it and
**Clear crop area** removes it.

<Screenshot id="crop-menu" alt="The Crop area menu open under the source card, with Change crop area and Clear crop area, and the crop region outlined on the preview." width={520} />

## Quick Shot

With **Quick Shot** there is no window to choose. Press the Scan shortcut (or **Draw and scan**) and
draw an area anywhere on the screen; Kimochi reads it once. Auto OCR isn't available in Quick Shot.
