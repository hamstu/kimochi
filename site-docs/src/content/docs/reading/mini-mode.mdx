**Mini mode** shrinks Kimochi to just its controls: your source, Auto OCR, the overlay, your OCR
model and dictionary, in a slim window that stays on top beside your source. Expand it again whenever
you want the Log.

- Press the **Mini mode** button in the main window's title bar (next to the sidebar button); it
  becomes **Expand Kimochi**.
- Or choose **Mini Mode** from the tray icon's menu.
- On macOS, Mini mode shows on top of an app in full screen.

<Screenshot id="mini-mode" alt="Kimochi in Mini mode: a narrow window with the source card, Auto OCR and Show overlay switches, OCR model and dictionary." width={260} />

Expanding restores the window's full size.

## When the game goes full screen

Kimochi doesn't shrink on its own. When what you're reading goes full screen **on the same screen
as Kimochi**, Kimochi keeps its size and stays on top of the game until the game leaves full screen.
Unpin it (the pin in its title bar) if you'd rather it went behind the game. On another screen,
nothing changes.

To have Kimochi switch to Mini mode instead, turn on **Settings → General → Full-screen games →
Switch to mini mode** (off by default). It then shrinks to Mini mode in a corner when the game goes
full screen on Kimochi's screen, once per time the game goes full screen: if you expand it, it stays
expanded.
