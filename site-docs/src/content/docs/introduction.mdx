Kimochi is a free, open-source desktop app that helps you read Japanese in games. It captures the
Japanese text on your screen with OCR, can draw that text back over the game as an overlay, and
lets you hover any word to look it up in your favourite dictionary and save it to Anki. It works on
games, visual novels, manga, YouTube videos and anything else on your screen.

It is built to stay beside the game rather than be a study app: a few small windows that keep out
of the way while you read.

<Screenshot id="main-window-tour" alt="The main window with numbered parts: 1 the source card, 2 the Auto OCR and Show overlay switches, 3 the OCR model, 4 the dictionary, 5 the Log and Notebook tabs, 6 a line in the Log, 7 the reading stats." width={900} />

1. **Source**: the window or screen Kimochi reads, with a live preview, **Crop area** and **Change source**.
2. **Auto OCR** and **Show overlay**: scan whenever the text changes, and draw it over the game.
3. **OCR model**: the engine that turns the capture into text.
4. **Dictionary**: the pop-up dictionary engine used for lookups.
5. **Log** and **Notebook**: the two views of the main window.
6. **A line**: every scan adds its text to the Log, with the time it arrived.
7. **Reading stats**: reading time, lines, characters and speed for this session.

## Words used in this guide

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Source | The window or screen Kimochi reads, or another input: Quick Shot, Clipboard or WebSocket. |
| Crop area | Part of the source to read, such as the dialogue box. |
| Scan | One OCR pass over the source (or the crop area). |
| Auto OCR | Scanning on its own whenever the text on screen changes and settles. |
| Overlay | The recognised text drawn over the game, so words can be looked up in place. |
| Log | The running transcript of every line, per session. |
| Session | A separate Log, Notebook and stats for one game or book. |
| Notebook | Characters, places and things of a session, recognised in the Log. |
| Controller menu | The radial menu over the game, opened with a shortcut or a gamepad chord. |
| Mini mode | The main window shrunk to its controls. |

## Platforms

Kimochi runs on macOS (Apple silicon), Windows (x64 and ARM64) and Linux (x64 and ARM64), and has a
Gaming Mode for the Steam Deck. macOS support is the most mature, and Steam Deck Gaming Mode is in
early testing. See [Installation](/install/).

Kimochi is early: it has nightly pre-release builds, made by one developer.

## Free, with an optional Kimochi Cloud

The whole app works without an account: capture, the overlay, Auto OCR, every OCR engine except
cloud OCR, the Log, Sessions, the Notebook, dictionaries, Anki and Gaming Mode. **Kimochi Cloud** is
an optional paid plan that adds in-app translation, AI explanations, read aloud, cloud OCR and sync
between computers. See [Accounts and plans](/cloud/).
