---
title: Controller buttons
description: Every gamepad button Kimochi uses, in the controller menu, while reading word by word, and in its windows.
---

Button names follow an Xbox-style layout: **A** is the bottom face button, **B** the right one.
**A**, **B**, **X**, **Y**, **LB**, **RB** and **View** can be changed in
**Settings → Shortcuts & Controls** (**Controller menu** and **Reading**); the D-pad and sticks are
fixed. On a Steam Deck in Gaming Mode, Kimochi also sees the four back buttons (**L4**, **R4**,
**L5**, **R5**) and the trackpad clicks, so you can bind any of them instead, and leave buttons
your game uses alone. Steam passes a back button to the game only if the game's Steam controller
layout binds it.

| Button | Controller menu | Word-by-word reading | Kimochi windows |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **LS + RS** (click both sticks; **R4** on a Steam Deck) | Open / close | Close | — |
| **A** (Select) | Choose the item | Into the pop-up (or look the word up again); there, press the chosen button. Full screen with the Yomitan API: add to Anki | Activate the focused control |
| **B** (Back) | Close | Back to the game (or to Recent text); from the pop-up, back to the text | Close a dialog, go back |
| **Y** (Word or character) | — | Step by word or by character | — |
| **X** (Play audio) | — | Play the word's audio | — |
| **LB** / **RB** | — | Previous / next definition (full screen: entry) | — |
| **View** (Show all controls) | — | Every control, over the game | — |
| **D-pad** | Step to an item | ◀ ▶ word, ▲ ▼ line; in the pop-up, choose a button | Move focus |
| **Left stick** | Aim at an item | A free cursor that looks up the character it rests on | Move focus |
| **Right stick** | — | Scroll the pop-up | Scroll |

Held directions repeat. Optional extras:

- A gamepad button or combination for **Scan** (**Settings → Shortcuts & Controls → Shortcuts**).
- **Scan after a button press**: scan a moment after the button that advances your game's text
  (**Settings → OCR & Overlay → Auto-scan**).
