Install Kimochi once from Desktop Mode. After that it lives in your Steam library, and you can open
it in Gaming Mode right alongside your game. Steam Deck support is early, and Gaming Mode is
experimental.

<Steps>

1. **Switch to Desktop Mode.** Press the **Steam** button, choose **Power**, then **Switch to Desktop**.

2. **Open Konsole.** Open the app launcher in the bottom-left corner, then go to **System** and
   choose **Konsole**. It's the terminal, where you'll paste one line.

3. **Paste the installer and press Enter.**

   ```sh
   curl -fsSL https://kimochiapp.com/install.sh | sh
   ```

   It's easiest if you open this page on the Deck itself. To paste in Konsole, press
   <kbd>Ctrl+Shift+V</kbd>, or right-click (the left trigger) and choose
   **Paste**. <kbd>Steam</kbd> + <kbd>X</kbd> brings up the on-screen keyboard. The installer never
   touches SteamOS's read-only system partition.

4. **Add Kimochi to your Steam library.** Kimochi opens when it's installed. Its welcome guide offers
   to add it to your Steam library, so it shows up in Gaming Mode with its own artwork. Say yes.

5. **Read in Desktop Mode or in Gaming Mode.** You can keep using Kimochi in Desktop Mode. Or
   double-click **Return to Gaming Mode** on the desktop and launch Kimochi from your library. It runs
   alongside your game: click both sticks to open its radial menu.

</Steps>

## Adding to Steam later

Kimochi adds itself as a non-Steam game, with its own library artwork. Nothing else in Steam changes,
and you can remove it from Steam like any other game. If you skipped the step, use
**Settings → General → Steam library → Add to Steam**. Its artwork appears once Steam restarts, as it
does when you switch to Gaming Mode.

<Screenshot id="settings-steam" alt="Settings, General page, with the Steam library section and its Add to Steam button." width={720} />

If Steam isn't running, open Steam from the desktop and try again. Or add it yourself: in Steam,
choose **Add a Game → Add a Non-Steam Game**, tick **Kimochi**, then **Add Selected Programs**.
